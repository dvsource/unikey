# Delivery plan and pilot readiness

_18 November 2026_

I would keep mid-January 2027 as the target for the eight-week Founding 100 pilot and use an earlier, small rehearsal to decide whether that target is credible. The emerging critical path is a reviewed pack feeding the real student loop, followed by reliable measurement and onboarding. The roadmap's widget, term-test mode and other optional surfaces compete with that path. Treat the list below as a proposed delivery sequence. ## Where I would spend the remaining build time

- **Use released content as a constraint.** Maintain a per-skill checklist covering approved Sinhala wording, an easy recovery item, a fresh application item, feedback and figures. Review on a representative low-cost Android phone. Include a versioned sign-off and a way to withdraw a faulty item. The three sample packs total 241 items according to the app QA report. That demonstrates authoring throughput. Review status, repair coverage and repeat exposure determine which parts are suitable for eight weeks of practice. [App QA](../../../app/qa/test-report.md) · [Mechanics review](../../../content/skills/mechanics/review.md) · [Current Electricity review](../../../content/skills/current-electricity/review.md) · [Thermal Physics review](../../../content/skills/thermal-physics/review.md)
- **Keep simulation focused on engineering acceptance.** Compare repair difficulty, skip behavior, session endings, participation and learning traces across the weak-impatient and fast-guesser personas, alongside the broader persona set. Record pack version, engine revision, seed and configuration. Use direct code assertions for rules with definite outcomes. Use student observation and pilot data for engagement and learning claims. [DR-0051](../../decisions/0051-study-loop-rules-after-first-judge-pass.md) · [Judging guide](../../../engine/judge/JUDGING.md)
- **Give each pilot capability a minimal operational path.** Prioritize teacher topic and assignment visibility, student identity and consent, practice events, activation and support. Confirm who operates manual tasks and how they reconcile mistakes. If parent reporting ships, test expiry and revocation before sending a link with student information. Keep later features out of the critical path unless a pilot hypothesis depends on them. ## Measurement decisions to record before enrollment

Keep the accepted rollout thresholds in DR-0009. Specify the D30 window, qualifying practice event, cohort denominator and treatment of dropouts. Report study days both across the enrolled cohort and among active students. Define active teacher use as an observable action and choose the reporting window. Record paid activation, refund eligibility, request and outcome separately.

Agree what “clear positive gain” means before seeing outcomes. Use comparable unseen week-0 and week-8 assessments scoped to taught pilot material, report matched-pair results and missing follow-ups, and track class, stream, prior exposure and practice frequency. If a comparison class is available, record selection differences and interpret score changes with those differences in view. Report the pilot offer conversion and paid continuation separately. [Accepted exit criteria](../../decisions/0009-pilot-exit-criteria.md) · [E01](../../experiments/E01-founding-100-pilot.md)

If the technical rehearsal is ready for only one taught section, run a small explicitly scoped rehearsal and retain the three-unit paid offer for when its content and operations are ready. If the paid pilot misses the accepted rollout criteria, use the measured failure to choose what to fix before widening teacher distribution.
