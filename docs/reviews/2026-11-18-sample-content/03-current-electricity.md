# Current Electricity sample review

**Verdict.** The treatment of induction and transformers belongs in this sample. The NIE Grade 13 guide explicitly includes them in competency 8.6, even though some source questions carry a magnetic-field tag in ALPhysics. The priority fixes are the Faraday-law wording and circuit questions whose connections determine the correct answer.

| **phy.elec.c038** | The stem asks what the induced emf equals, the keyed words give the rate of change of flux linkage, and the explanation gives its negative. A student following the signed formula can reasonably object. Source question 2025 Q9 asks for a numerical average emf with a specified coil orientation. | Ask “ප්‍රේරිත විද්‍යුත් ගාමක බලයේ විශාලත්වය කුමකට සමාන ද?” Key “චුම්බක ස්‍රාව බන්ධනය වෙනස් වීමේ ශීඝ්‍රතාවයේ විශාලත්වයට.” Show the signed expression alongside a sentence relating the minus sign to Lenz’s law. |

| **phy.elec.p01-06** | Two opposing cells and three resistors can form a single loop. For that layout, the net emf and total resistance suffice. | **phy.elec.c043** | “පුඩුවක් ඒ අසල සමතලාව ඇත” does not specify the orientation relative to the wire. A loop can be flat and have zero flux from that wire. | “සංවෘත සන්නායක පුඩුවක් හා දිගු සෘජු කම්බියක් එකම තලයේ පිහිටයි. පුඩුව සම්පූර්ණයෙන්ම කම්බියේ එක් පැත්තක ඇත.” Keep the decreasing current. The induced field then supports the original flux through the loop. |

**Examples to retain.** The 2 mA key in c035 follows directly from the balance condition. A 10 mV drop over 50 cm gives 20 mV across the uniform 100 cm wire and 0.020 V / 10 Ω = 0.002 A. The four orientation words in the induction pool matter. When the field into the page strengthens, the induced field points out of the page, hence anticlockwise as viewed from the front. The existing explicit viewing direction in p02-01 makes that choice defensible.

**Checks.** The 82 items cite 20 distinct references. All resolve in ALPhysics, across Units 7 and 8 in its index. The official guide locates electrical power in 8.2, sources in 8.3, circuits in 8.4, meters and bridges in 8.5, and induction in 8.6. All 988 stored numerical answers across 13 families agreed with independent calculations. All 43 generated statement combinations matched the marked Sinhala source statements. The checks use the existing sample files and selected past-paper records. They do not constitute a page-by-page comparison of ten printed papers or native-teacher sign-off on every sentence.
