# Competitors

_Last updated: 2026-11-02 (from web research in the source chat; re-verify quarterly)_

## Where the white space is
Nobody is doing **spaced-repetition, mastery-based daily practice that is teacher-neutral and comes with class analytics**. Everyone else does content delivery (videos, LMS) or Q&A chat.

**Watch closely:** AP.LK / LearniX (direct physics competitor) and BrainUs's gamified launch (BrainUs Learn).

Rule: a new competitor changes the plan **only if it hits a revisit trigger** in a decision record ([DR-0016](../decisions/0016-stop-only-by-decision.md)). Competitors appearing means the market is real.

## A. AI tutors and AI-notes apps (closest to us)
| Player | What it is | Threat |
|---|---|---|
| BrainUs LK | AI tutor on SL textbooks, syllabuses and past papers. ~2,000 students via word of mouth, free tier. Piloting **BrainUs Learn**, a gamified curriculum platform. 41%+ found it via ChatGPT-style AI recommendations. | 🔴 High: same timing, same gamification angle |
| Apilage AI | AI-generated A/L short notes aligned to the syllabus | 🟠 Medium |
| GradiBee | AI tutors, syllabus lessons, papers, progress tracking in 3 mediums, grades 5–13, parent layer | 🟠 Medium: has a parent angle |
| SriAI | Claude-based chat for the NIE syllabus and past papers, LKR bank transfer | 🟡 Low: generic chat, no study system |
| Nenasa AI (government) | Free AI assistant on e-thaksalawa, 3 languages | 🟡 Low quality, but free and official |

## B. Teacher-led platforms (most important group)
| Player | Notes |
|---|---|
| **AP.LK / LearniX AI** | A/L Physics platform, claims to be the first AI LMS, 70,000+ students. 🔴 **Most direct physics competitor.** Study closely. |
| DM Education | A/L Biology, claims 100,000+ students. Shows how one teacher brand scales. |
| EDUS | Online tuition + app, founded 2021, 7,000+ students |
| A/L Kuppiya (SLT-Mobitel) | Video-on-demand, live classes, store. Telco-backed. |
| Charith Sir app | Example of a teacher with their own app (A/L ICT) |

Their structural weakness: they serve their own students only. Rival teachers won't send students to them. We are teacher-neutral.

## C. White-label tools for tutors
Edumix (branded LMS + app per institute), LimeCodeLabs, Tuto. These do video, live classes, fee collection and attendance, **not spaced repetition or mastery**. This is a realistic copy risk (flashcards are easy for them; a verified skill graph and template bank are not). If they copy us, they could become a licensing customer.

## D. Past paper and quiz apps
- QuizUp Sri Lanka: reportedly #1 in Education on both stores within 24 hours. Evidence of demand for gamified quizzes.
- ExamHub (gamified past-paper practice), Examiner ($5.99/yr premium), AlevelApi, Nova Learn.
- epapers.lk and others: every A/L and O/L paper 2000–2025 in all mediums, **free**. A past-paper bank is not a product.

## E. Global and free
ChatGPT, Gemini, Anki, Quizlet, YouTube.

## Price anchors
- Apps: ~2,000 LKR/year (Examiner $5.99/yr).
- Tuition: one class per subject per month costs more than 900 LKR. **This is our anchor.**
