# DR-0012: Sell Physics as a single subject; Bio-stream students are the first segment

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-02 · **Owner:** Viraj
- **Area:** Positioning / Pricing

## Context
Concern: will students and parents pay for a physics-only product? In SL, tuition is bought per subject; single-subject platforms (AP.LK, DM Education) have scaled. Bio-stream students fear physics, and it drags their Z-score toward medicine.

## Options considered
1. Wait for multiple subjects before charging: delays revenue and learning
2. **Single subject, positioned as the fix for the weak subject; Bio students first** ✅
3. Position as all-in-one: false today

## Decision
- Physics sold alone. Positioning for Bio: *"Physics is the subject that decides medicine. 15 minutes a day to stop it pulling your Z-score down."*
- A "Physics for Bio students" path (same content; different default mix, more scaffolding, more L1–L3).
- **One app, subjects as add-ons**, never separate apps. Plan bundles (1 = 900, 2 = 1,500, stream = 2,000). Founding discounts extend to future subjects.

## Assumptions
- [A8](../experiments/assumptions.md): Bio-stream students convert better
- [A9](../experiments/assumptions.md): parents pay 900/month for one subject

## Revisit triggers
- Pilot data: Bio and Physical Science conversion/retention are equal → drop the Bio-first messaging
- Review date: 2027-04-01

## Consequences
+ A sharp message for the most motivated payers
− A narrower initial audience
