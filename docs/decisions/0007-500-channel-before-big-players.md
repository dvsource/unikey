# DR-0007: Use the wife's 500-teacher WhatsApp channel before big tuition players

- **Status:** Superseded by DR-0008 (for the pilot phase; the channel order after the pilot still holds)
- **Date:** 2026-11-02 · **Owner:** Viraj · **Consulted:** wife
- **Area:** GTM

## Context
Two channel options: a WhatsApp channel of 500+ government physics teachers the wife belongs to (warm access, small classes), or big tuition players (huge reach, cold, gatekept, high bargaining power).

## Options considered
1. **500 government teachers first** ✅: warm, fast, honest varied feedback, low copy risk, a source of reviewers for other mediums
2. Big tuition players first: needs proof we don't have yet; they dictate terms

## Decision
Start with the 500-teacher channel: opt-in only (admin permission → personal post → form → call those who opt in). No cold-calling the numbers. Then mid-size tuition teachers, then big players with outcome proof.

## Assumptions
- [A12](../experiments/assumptions.md): ~10–20% of channel teachers opt in

## Revisit triggers
- (Superseded: the pilot moved to the close circle in DR-0008)

## Consequences
+ The channel doubles as the paid reviewer pool for Tamil/English mediums
− Government teacher conduct rules complicate commercial models (see DR-0011)
