# DR-0001: Launch with A/L Physics only

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Owner:** Viraj · **Consulted:** wife (A/L Physics teacher)
- **Area:** Product / Content

## Context
The long-term vision covers every A/L subject in three mediums. Content quality is the main trust risk, and we have exactly one expert reviewer in-house: a practising A/L Physics teacher.

## Options considered
1. All science subjects at launch: bigger offer, but no reviewer capacity and weaker quality
2. **Physics first** ✅: in-house expertise, a big cohort (Bio + Physical Science streams take it), and Bio students fear it
3. Biology first: best spaced-repetition fit, but no in-house reviewer

## Decision
Launch with Physics only. Subject order afterwards: Chemistry → Biology → Combined Maths ([roadmap](../living/roadmap.md#subject-order)).

## Assumptions
- [A9](../experiments/assumptions.md): parents will pay for a single-subject product

## Revisit triggers
- The pilot parent survey shows <30% would pay for Physics alone
- A second in-house-quality reviewer becomes available for another subject
- Review date: 2027-04-01

## Consequences
+ Content quality is achievable; we go deep before going wide
− Smaller offer; the parent "only physics?" objection (answer: "we do one subject properly; chemistry next")
