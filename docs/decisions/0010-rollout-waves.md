# DR-0010: Onboard channel teachers in waves (15 → 30 → rest), medium-matched

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-02 · **Owner:** Viraj
- **Area:** GTM

## Context
~75 opted-in teachers × ~50 students ≈ 3,000–4,000 students: a 30–40× jump in support, bugs and content demand over the pilot.

## Options considered
1. Onboard everyone at once: maximum momentum, likely breakage
2. **Waves: 15 teachers → 2–3 weeks fixing → 30 → 2–3 weeks → rest** ✅

## Decision
Waves, prioritising teachers whose medium our content covers. The others are waitlisted and kept warm with monthly updates (screenshot, pilot stat, early-access date). The rollout requires most of the syllabus in at least one medium, so the content pipeline runs in parallel with the pilot.

## Revisit triggers
- Wave 1 support load is easily manageable → merge waves 2 and 3
- Content coverage isn't ready → delay the waves, don't onboard onto thin content
- Review date: 2027-04-15

## Consequences
+ Controlled growth, fixes between waves
− Some keen teachers wait; the waitlist needs active warming
