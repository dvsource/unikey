# DR-0035: Paid teacher reviewers for the pilot's Electricity and Thermal units

- **Status:** Accepted (Viraj, 2026-11-07, picked from options)
- **Date:** 2026-11-07 · **Owner:** Viraj · **Consulted:** wife (A/L Physics teacher)
- **Area:** Content / Team

## Context
The pilot covers 3 units ([DR-0031](0031-pilot-scope-3-units-sinhala.md)). The wife can give **under 5 hours a week** until January, which covers about one unit. Every item must be teacher-reviewed before students see it ([DR-0027](0027-content-plan-stop-point.md)).

## Options considered
1. **Paid teacher reviewers for units 2 and 3** ✅
2. Pilot with fewer units
3. Lighter review (templates and tags only, 10% card spot-check). Higher error risk in front of students.
4. Move the pilot later

## Decision
- The wife reviews **Mechanics** fully and owns the glossary and the final sign-off.
- **1–2 paid Sinhala-medium A/L physics teachers** from her circle review **Current Electricity** and **Thermal Physics**. She spot-checks their work.
- Reviewers follow the spec's review format: templates and tags fully, about 30% of fixed cards spot-checked ([content authoring §10](../living/content-authoring.md#10-workflow-and-statuses)).
- Each reviewer signs a simple IP assignment before starting ([legal](../living/legal-and-privacy.md)).
- **Still open:** who, the pay rate (per item or per hour), and the start date. Recruit them in November.

## Revisit triggers
- No reviewer recruited by 2026-11-30
- Spot-checks find many errors in a reviewer's work
- The DR-0031 check fires (units 2–3 not reviewed by mid-December)
- Review date: 2027-01-15

## Consequences
+ 3 units stay realistic without moving the pilot
+ Builds the reviewer pool needed later for more units and mediums
− A new cost before any revenue
− Terminology consistency across reviewers depends on the wife's glossary
