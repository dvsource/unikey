# DR-0017: Share the plan with a business-minded friend as an informal advisor (no equity yet)

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-02 · **Owner:** Viraj
- **Area:** Company

## Context
"Don't jinx it" has a basis (Gollwitzer, 2009): announcing goals for validation reduces effort. Telling for critique and accountability helps, especially given the abandonment pattern. A friend with a strong business mindset in tech is available.

## Options considered
1. Tell no one: no critique, no accountability
2. Broadcast widely: the validation effect, no progress
3. **Talk to one sharp critic with the full plan; ask for criticism; end with a dated commitment** ✅

## Decision
Option 3. Bring the plan (positioning, pilot, exit criteria, pricing, competitors, teacher model) and ask "tell me why this fails." Specific questions: price and plan type, the teachers-never-sell model, payments/legal/company setup, "would you pay as a parent?", who else to talk to. Commit: "I'll show you pilot numbers by March." **No equity or partnership in the first conversation.** Informal advisor first; decide roles after the pilot. No broad announcements until the pilot is running.

## Revisit triggers
- After the pilot: decide whether a formal role (likely sales/marketing) is needed → new DR
- Review date: 2027-04-01

## Consequences
+ Critique and accountability
− Some idea-exposure risk (low: execution is the moat)
