# DR-0011: Teachers never sell. Free tool for teachers; premium sold to parents

- **Status:** Superseded by [DR-0034](0034-teacher-offer-free-tool-plus-affiliate.md)
- **Date:** 2026-11-02 · **Owner:** Viraj
- **Area:** GTM / Pricing

## Context
Viraj's draft teacher offer led with stats, a revenue share (50 LKR/student), teaching materials, a paper builder and admin help. Government teachers face MoE circulars restricting charging their own school students. A revenue share makes them look like sales agents, triggers "what's the catch?", and 50 LKR looks small in public. Teachers are skeptical and busy.

## Options considered
1. Teachers sell / earn affiliate on all students: aligned incentives, but ethical and rule friction for government teachers
2. Bundle into tuition fees: works for big private batches only
3. **Teachers get the tool free forever; we sell premium to parents directly; teacher earning is opt-in, 1:1 only** ✅

## Decision
- Teachers: free forever (paper builder, materials, admin report, dashboard).
- Students in a teacher's class: free basic tier.
- Premium: sold to parents by us. The teacher at most mentions it.
- Earning options (offered 1:1, never in the first message): paid content reviewer; affiliate share only for *private tuition* students, where the rules allow it.
- Lead the pitch with the **paper builder** ([go-to-market](../living/go-to-market.md#selling-points-in-order)).

## Assumptions
- [A10](../experiments/assumptions.md): teachers adopt without revenue share
- [A11](../experiments/assumptions.md): paper building is the most painful teacher task

## Revisit triggers
- Teacher opt-in rate <5% in the channel survey
- MoE circulars turn out to explicitly allow teacher affiliate income
- Review date: 2027-04-15

## Consequences
+ Zero ethical friction; teachers can adopt instantly
− We carry all the selling to parents; the paper builder becomes a must-ship before the channel rollout
