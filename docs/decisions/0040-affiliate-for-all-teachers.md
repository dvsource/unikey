# DR-0040: No affiliate share in the pilot or for government teachers

- **Status:** Accepted (Viraj, 2026-11-07)
- **Date:** 2026-11-07 · **Owner:** Viraj
- **Area:** GTM / Pricing
- **Source:** [teacher dashboard plan](../living/teacher-dashboard.md), rev 2. An earlier draft ("share for every teacher, pilot included") was reversed by Viraj after review.
- **Confirms:** [DR-0034](0034-teacher-offer-free-tool-plus-affiliate.md)'s restriction (private-tuition students only, once L2 is answered)

## Context
DR-0034 gives teachers a 100 LKR/month affiliate share per paying student, limited to private-tuition students until legal question L2 (MoE rules on government teachers) is answered. The pilot teachers are mostly government school teachers, and one is Viraj's wife.

## Options considered
1. Share for every teacher, pilot included (first draft)
2. **No share in the pilot or for government teachers; DR-0034 stands** ✅

## Decision
- **No affiliate share in the pilot**, and none for government teachers on their school students.
- After the pilot, the share applies to private-tuition students only, and only once L2 is answered.
- **Pilot teachers get instead:** free premium, a Founding Teacher credit, and optional paid reviewing ([DR-0035](0035-paid-teacher-reviewers-pilot.md)).
- The Earnings screen, the ledger and payouts are out of the pilot.

## Why
- A government teacher earning from a paid app they recommend to the students they also grade is the conflict MoE circulars target. One complaint could hurt the wife's standing and the brand in the 500-teacher channel before launch.
- One pilot teacher is the wife; paying a family business share teaches nothing.
- 200 of the 300 LKR pilot price would go to the share, for no learning.

## Revisit triggers
- L2 is answered (either way)
- Pilot teachers say they won't recommend it without a share
- Review date: 2027-04-01

## Consequences
+ No conflict-of-interest risk during the pilot; simpler build
− Teachers' only reward in the pilot is non-cash
