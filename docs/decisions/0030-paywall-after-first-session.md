# DR-0030: Paywall after the first session; class-code students pay the same

- **Status:** Accepted (Viraj, 2026-11-07, picked from options); order settled by [DR-0055](0055-onboarding-revised-for-anki-loop.md): paywall after sign-in
- **Date:** 2026-11-07 · **Owner:** Viraj
- **Area:** Pricing / Product
- **Follows:** [DR-0029](0029-no-free-plan.md), which left both questions open

## Context
With no free plan, two things were open: how much a new student uses before paying, and whether students who join with a teacher's class code get anything free.

## Options considered
Before paying:
1. **Onboarding, quick check and the first 5-minute session free, then pay** ✅
2. A 3-day trial of everything
3. Pay before onboarding

Class-code students:
1. **Pay the same as everyone; the teacher can share a discount code (e.g. the 800 referral/bulk price)** ✅
2. A free first week
3. Free while linked to a class

## Decision
- **Paywall after A6 (first session done).** The student gets the first win ([DR-0026](0026-onboarding-flow.md)) and sees their starting map and readiness change, then pays to continue. The paywall offers "Ask a parent" on WhatsApp ([DR-0018](0018-student-is-primary-customer.md)).
- Order is still to be designed: the paywall sits between A6 and A7 (save progress) or right after A7.
- **Class-code students pay the same.** Teachers can share a discount code; teachers themselves stay free ([DR-0011](0011-teachers-never-sell.md)).
- The pilot is unchanged: Founding 100 pay 300 LKR up front ([DR-0003](0003-pilot-price-300.md)).

## Revisit triggers
- Most students drop at the paywall without tapping "Ask a parent"
- Teachers say students won't try an app they must pay for after one session
- Review date: 2027-04-01

## Consequences
+ The first win still comes before any payment
+ One price rule for everyone; simple to explain
− One session may be too little to show value, so the first session must be good
