## Hypothesis
If the wife posts a personal, benefit-first invite (paper builder, materials, class progress; no price, no revenue share) in the 500-teacher WhatsApp channel, 10–20% will opt in, and "building term-test papers" will be the top pain.

## Setup
- Ask the **channel admin for permission** first.
- Post the [first message](../living/go-to-market.md#whatsapp-teacher-channel-first-message-wifes-voice-translate-to-sinhala-no-price-no-revenue-share) in Sinhala. List only the features that will exist at rollout.
- The 5-question [opt-in form](../living/go-to-market.md#teacher-opt-in-form-5-questions).
- Call only the people who opt in (15 min each) and pick 10–20 best fits (a mix of medium, region and school type).
- Keep the waitlist warm: a monthly update (screenshot, pilot stat, early-access date).

## Metrics & thresholds
| Metric | Success | Rethink |
|---|---|---|
| Opt-in rate | ≥ 10% (50+) | < 5% → revisit the offer/message (DR-0011) |
| Top pain = paper building | Most common answer | Another pain dominates → lead with that |
| Paid reviewer interest (Tamil/English) | ≥ 5 Yes | 0 → look for reviewers elsewhere |
