# Thermal Physics coverage report (draft)

> **Paper I MCQs only.** Paper II (structured and essay) is not mapped yet. Map it before treating any weight here as final.

Generated by `author.py`. Real papers 2016–2025 only, with no `predict-2026` and no model papers. 58 questions are mapped to 46 syllabus skills. Weights are worked out in three steps:

1. **Skill score** = primary hits + 0.5 × secondary hits.
2. **Level weight** = 0.5 × the level's share of the unit's score + 0.5 × its share of teaching periods.
3. **Within a level**, each skill gets max(0.1, score ÷ the level's top score). These are normalised, then multiplied by the level weight to give the skill's **unit share**.

**Draft weight** (`exam_weight`) = unit share ÷ the largest unit share.
